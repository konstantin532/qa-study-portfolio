# QA Study Portfolio

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Russian-speaking people studying manual software testing and building a collection of their work. Inferred from existing course and README; not a newly confirmed audience.

## Product Purpose
A local learning workspace connecting a 23-module course, 69 topics, 302 questions, notes, spaced repetition, practical artifacts and a portfolio.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Keep all existing main routes, course content and identifiers. Keep IndexedDB database qa-study-portfolio version 1 and its localStorage fallback. No frameworks, build step or online dependency; opening index.html via file:// must continue to work. Preserve light/dark/system themes and keyboard access. No migration, account, telemetry or production deployment in this change.

## Brand Commitments
The user requests a much more distinctive, innovative, impressive visual experience and considers the current appearance inadequate. Existing prototypes are reference material, not a binding visual direction. Keep QA Study Portfolio as the product name. The Fieldwork editorial direction is the implementer's assumption, open to user correction.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing application and four standalone HTML concepts. Their learning mechanics and data differ; do not merge their datasets or make mastery claims from quiz scores. All metrics in the application must come from loaded data and user state.

## Product Principles
- Make the next useful action obvious.
- Let completed work and actual learning progress carry the story.
- Keep long reading and editing calm and legible.
- Store learning data locally and retain export/import.
